# CustodIA

Chat-native agentic finance runtime for ETHOnline 2026.

The agent proposes. The human signs a boundary. A deterministic policy engine enforces it. Chat is transport; the task belongs to the wallet ENS name.

**What it is not:** an AI trading bot, real-money custody, or unbounded agent permissions.

**Demo scene:** Sepolia + Hedera testnet. Execution is simulated and labeled as such.

**Primary action:** open `/chat`, sign the conversation, claim `{label}.custodia.eth`. Market questions stay ephemeral. Protection and guards become ENS tasks.

**Live channel:** web chat. Telegram is not in this build.

**Proof:** generated UX at `{owner}.custodia.eth/{task-hash}/{task-name}`, live Graph data, x402-paid risk, ENSv2 records.
